# DiscordSync terms of use

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

## Scope

DiscordSync is a personal support helper operated by helix4u. It is used to review
public Discord support-thread conversations, investigate reported issues, and
maintain support records. It is currently used by one operator and is not offered
as a public installation or subscription service.

## Support use

The helper assists the operator's investigation. Automated output can contain
mistakes and does not guarantee a diagnosis, fix, response time, or resolution.
The operator remains responsible for assessing evidence and deciding what support
response or action is appropriate.

## Discord and third-party services

DiscordSync operates subject to Discord's applicable terms and policies. Using
this helper does not replace Discord's rules or the terms of any AI service used
for troubleshooting. DiscordSync is not endorsed by or affiliated with Discord.

## Content and privacy

Support-thread content remains subject to its authors' rights. Copying information
for support work does not transfer ownership to the operator. Data handling and
requests to correct or remove copies are described in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Changes and contact

The helper can be changed or discontinued as the operator's support workflow
changes. Material updates to these documents will be reflected in their update
dates. Questions about the helper or these terms can be raised directly with
helix4u in the relevant support conversation.
